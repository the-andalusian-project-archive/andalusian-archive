{%- comment -%}
  2026-09-28, homepage order. The page used to open with a stats panel and then
  an institutional-affiliations line, and its most prominent "about" link was
  described as holding "every attested form of his name".

  Three things were wrong with that, and only the first was about SEO.

  1. A reader arriving from a search engine arrives with a question, not a name,
     and the page gave them neither. It opened on arithmetic.
  2. The hero subtitle listed his institutional affiliations - research fellow
     at this institute, member of that body - in the first twenty words. That is
     a credential pitch to a reader who came to read an argument, and it is
     biography the site has no stake in asserting.
  3. "Every attested form of his name" is a name-resolution summary, and it was
     the fifth card on the front page. A page that aggregates every identifier for
     a living person who has asked for a private life should not be what a
     homepage advertises. The page still exists and still matters - it stops his
     work being confused with another man also called Al-Andalusi - but it is
     demoted, and the homepage no longer sells it as a feature.

  So: a short plain introduction, then the questions the work answers, then the
  collections, then the detail. The affiliation line is gone from the front page
  entirely; it is on the "Who this is" page, which is where a reader goes when
  they want it.
{%- endcomment -%}

<div class="section intro-section">
  <p>A writer and lecturer named Asadullah Ali Al-Andalusi ran a website and a
  YouTube channel called <em>The Andalusian Project</em>. Both are gone: the
  domain name now belongs to an unrelated gambling operation, and the channel is
  unavailable. This is what survives &mdash; his writing, his papers, his
  recordings and their transcripts &mdash; rescued from web archives, with a record
  of where every item came from and whether any text is actually held.</p>
  <p><strong>He has left online dawah, has asked not to be contacted, and the archive does not contact
  him.</strong> The point of preserving this is so the work can be read, cited and
  taught from.</p>
</div>

    <p class="taxonomy-detail">A debate he produced, an interview he conducted and a talk he gave at someone else&rsquo;s hall are all A: he made the recording. The {{ mdi_republished }} MDI rows that republish a work already in this list are catalogued but not counted again.</p>
