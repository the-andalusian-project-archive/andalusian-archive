# The Andalusian Project Archive

**A preservation archive of the published work of Asadullah Ali Al-Andalusi, founder of The
Andalusian Project** — openly available, preserved with provenance.

## What this account maintains

- **A recovered archive of a web presence that no longer exists.** `asadullahali.com` served an
  unrelated gambling operation when it was last checked on 2026-09-27, his WordPress mirror has
  had its post bodies removed, and his YouTube channel returned "This channel is not available."
  at the live check on the same date. Everything the archive holds was recovered from the Wayback
  Machine, the Internet Archive, the Muslim Debate Initiative and his own surviving mirror —
  verbatim, with per-item provenance.
- **72 recovered works** — 47 with full text in the repository, 24 catalogued Wayback-only, 1
  unrecovered. Dated 2011-12-18 to 2020-08-11.
- **68 video records** — 56 preserved as files in the Internet Archive's `andalusian-project`
  collection, 12 catalogued as live re-uploads, with the channel's capture-dated history and
  the claims this archive refuses to make recorded alongside it.
- **68 published machine transcripts, 540,995 words** — one document per capture, each with its
  capture file, cue count, language and producing model. Every page carries the
  machine-transcript disclaimer.
- **20 papers** — 6 PDF files held locally across 5 of the paper records, with a 24-row access
  ledger recording what is open,
  what is restricted and what is dead, including one DOI whose publisher domain no longer
  resolves.
- **The evidence trail.** Six recovery lanes, an append-only integration log, two independent
  review rounds and their fix waves are committed in the archive repository under
  `docs/recovery-log/`, not left in someone's scratch directory.

## Framing

The material is **openly available material, archive and lost-and-found.** It was not lost so
much as scattered and unlinked: the best copies sat across Wayback replay URLs, an Internet
Archive directory listing, a live third-party site and an emptied WordPress shell. What did not
exist was one citable place, with a stated provenance for every item in it. That is what this
account maintains.

The author's works are reproduced verbatim with attribution and are **not relicensed**; the
archive's own data, scripts and documentation are CC BY-NC 4.0. See `LICENSE` and `NOTICE.md` in
the archive repository, which list every withheld item. This is a dated polemical archive: the
claims are the author's, not the archive's.
