{%- comment -%} Transcript section. Four cases, decided by the data, never by guesswork: 1. a coverage row for this video with captions, and the entry does not declare `transcripts: 0` -> the transcript exists AND its text is published. The disclaimer is printed VERBATIM as the brief requires, the capture's provenance is given, and the text is linked. The index is _data/transcript_index.json, written by scripts/build_collections.py :: build_transcripts(), keyed by the recording the capture transcribes - so a capture re-parented from a superseded duplicate upload is listed here as an ADDITIONAL transcript of this same recording, attributed to its own upload. 2. `transcripts: 0` -> a neutral "not yet available" note. No promise that one is coming, and no link to a file that is not there. Where a capture exists but is deliberately unattached, the note says so factually and still links nothing: that entry's page does not present a transcript, because the archive decided it should not. 3. a coverage row that names a file but has no captions flag -> the recording could not be captioned at all; the same neutral note. 4. a capture whose words are also printed with the essay it belongs to -> the article is linked as well, so a reader who came for the writing is not sent to a machine transcript instead. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign cov = site.data.transcript_coverage | where: "video_id", page.video_id | first -%} {%- assign tdocs = site.data.transcript_index.by_recording[page.video_id] -%}
