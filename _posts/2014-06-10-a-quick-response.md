# A Quick Response

Typically I reserve only a small amount of time to discuss religious or political issues on YouTube given the frivolous nature of the exchanges on these sort of forums. There is very little to learn, much less discuss on websites where the majority of users are more interested in sound bites and drama. I must admit that I’ve been guilty of getting into longer discussions when I felt a point was necessary to make, but in this instance I have once again proven to myself why I shouldn’t bother.
After an argument I had about the nature of apostasy in Islam in the comments section, one of the more active ex-Muslims on the site by the name of CaptainDisguise decided to put up a blog post in response.[1] There were no arguments or objections. It was simply a poor attempt at making a comedy out of my arguments while posturing, hoping that his audience might be receptive to the idea that I’m just some crazy extremist who isn’t worth listening to.
You would think then that my comments would speak for themselves without having to be highlighted and given absurd interpretations, but as we all know, sub par minds find just about everything funny when it doesn’t fit their narrow perception of reality.
While I will be eventually writing a full blog article on the subject of apostasy in Islam, I feel like proving a point with this quick response: the individuals who oppose this understanding are in fact worse than the people they accuse. So, without further ado, I will respond in similar style with some added substance.
This is CaptainDisguise. Don’t see anyone? Well, that’s the point. It’s a disguise. His real face and name are not revealed, because he and his ideas are so valuable in the battle against cave dwelling terrorists with big beards, that he is in constant danger of being killed. So he’s sort of like Batman, but braver because he doesn’t even have an alter ego – he fights crime behind a computer screen. Dangerous stuff. Thank him for his overwhelming contributions to the world later.
He begins by mentioning my name and showing a picture of me, claiming that I chose my name despite it having been given to me by others. He also says I’m ‘white’, when I’m actually a southern Spaniard – not that I have anything against white people, but you’d think an attempt at a racial joke could have been pulled off a little better. In the very least, I’m much browner than the people Captain hangs around with online. He also seems to think my degrees in philosophy and Islamic studies matters very little. While I admit it doesn’t make me a genius, it certainly makes me qualified to say something about the subjects themselves. But let’s be honest, a person who takes the YouTube personality “Klingschor” [1] as an authority in Islamic studies — a kid who hasn’t even finished a minor in the subject — wouldn’t have much appreciation for credentials far above that.
Mr. Disguise goes on to for the rest of the post to attempt to discredit my reasoning pertaining to the idea of punishment for apostasy in Islam, which in sum is simply the idea of ‘social contract’. People should be able to choose their laws and should be allowed to enter contracts and face the consequences for breaching them. In an Islamic polity the punishment for apostasy from Islam is most certainly death; this after three days have expired in attempts to reason with the apostate and to assess whether or not they left the faith due to coercion or mental illness. Prior to this, the apostate being charged must have first paid loyalty to the Islamic polity to begin with, knowing full well their responsibilities and the consequence of the act.
In other words, I do not believe that people like CaptainDisguise should be killed, not only for the reasons stated above, but also for numerous other things I made clear in the posts he’s attempting to make sport of. There is no Islamic polity, and many of the Muslims that are becoming apostates today have no idea about the consequences for apostasy, much less have they given allegiance to a state that is obligated to punish the act. CaptainDisguises entire post is an overreaction — no doubt surprising given his emotional investment and profound ignorance of the topic at hand. What makes matters more embarrassing is that one of his fellow atheist understood my argument well enough to know I was not advocating for murder,[3] but CaptainDisguise never quite got it.
However, the most important point in all this is that CaptainDisguise and many like him, while wailing against Islam and Muslims for their ideas — no matter how harmless — are much worse than the people and ideas they constantly attack. They are apologists for liberal fascism. What I mean by this is that they every problem in the world with a breach of contract being punished, but they have absolutely no problem with people being killed by secular societies for simply having different views or wanting to live a different way of life other than secular-materialist-consumerist culture. Constantly, other societies are being ravaged for merely disagreeing with the Western perception of reality, and laws and sanctions are being imposed on them for not wanting to be a part of the Western global hegemony. And you never hear a peep out of these people for the millions that have already been killed or suffer because of it. Why? Their silence is not due to a lack of awareness or because they have more important things to worry about — seeing as most of their time is devoted to the online world where they can stroke their egos — but because they are complicit to these atrocities. ‘Freedom’ for them means “follow my way or the highway” — and no contracts were ever agreed upon.
So you will only see blog responses and comments attacking Muslims and Islam — for the death of one or two individuals — but you will never see even the most minor criticism attacking the societies they promote as ‘civilized’ and ‘advanced’, despite the chaos they are creating across the world and the millions dead or dying.
But what can you expect from people who fantasize about their intellectual opponents blowing themselves up in a suicide attack? I mean, look at the smiley face. They hate terrorism and wish its end, but then glorify it when it comes to people they dislike. Only sick minds would be able to casually speak of such things without any remorse. And let’s be fair, when I was constantly around these people, I spoke just the same way. After finding how toxic the YouTube atheists community was in general, I decided to leave. Since then, I’ve purged myself of such behavior. But it certainly goes to show you who really should be held accountable. The Muslim who believes in man’s right to choose their religion, their laws, and submit themselves to contract, or the non-Muslims here who fantasize about their opponents being killed, and do and say nothing to stop their societies from killing others just because of mere disagreement? In any case I wish these people well, but I cannot say that they wish the same for me.
