{%- comment -%} "Related" block: 3-5 sibling links per detail page, derived from join keys the archive's own data already carries (2026-09-27, on-page SEO pass). WHY THIS EXISTS. Measured before this file: 27 of the archive's 311 pages had zero inbound internal links, and 20 of those were the entire academic-papers collection, because `/papers/` linked only to PDFs. The papers index is now fixed directly (papers.md), but the general defect is that a detail page offers the reader no onward path, so a crawler arriving at one of the 300 other detail pages has nothing to follow. This is the fix for all of them. WHAT COUNTS AS A RELATIONSHIP HERE. Every link below is a real, data-derived one, and the label on each link says which relation it is, so a reader can check the claim rather than take it: works shares a `tags` value, else shares a `categories` value, with another work. Both are read from the recovered posts' own front matter - the original site's own tag and category taxonomy. papers shares a named co-author, else shares a publishing venue, else shares a publication type, with another paper. videos shares a `themes` or `topics` value with another recording, both from `_data/videos.json`. transcripts the recording it transcribes; then any other transcript of the SAME recording; then, to fill out, recordings that share a theme or topic with the recording it transcribes. WHAT IS REFUSED. No cross-collection linking for its own sake: a paper is never linked from a video because they are both "on Islam". No link to a page that does not exist, and no link to a row without a published page - the candidate list is only ever walked when the target collection is one this archive actually publishes a page for. No corpus count appears in the block.
