What a machine transcript is, and is not

Each of these texts is speech-recognition output over the recording’s own captions. It is published exactly as the machine wrote it: no correction, no spell-checking, no smoothing, and paragraphs broken only on pauses of more than two seconds. It mishears names, loses negations and flattens argument, and it is printed with that sentence above it for a reason.

What it is good for is finding the passage. Across the {{ ix.documents }} transcripts in this archive — {{ ix.words }} words — this is the only full text of these talks that exists anywhere. A reader who wants to know what was argued in session three can read session three, and then go back to the recording for the parts the machine got wrong.

Series grouping is derived by scripts/build_series_index.py from the channel’s own NN - <series name> title shape, and the rule is re-checkable with --check. It is a view, not a category system: recordings that are not parts of a series are not filed under an invented one. See the series index for what is grouped and what is not, the full transcript list, and the entity page for who the author is.
