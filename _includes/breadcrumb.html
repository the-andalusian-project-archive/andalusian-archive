{%- comment -%} BreadcrumbList, emitted as its own ld+json block alongside the entity block (2026-09-27, on-page SEO pass). Emitted ONLY where the site's own navigation states the hierarchy. Every page in the site nav links to Home and to its collection, and every detail page carries a "Back to " link, so Home -> -> item is the hierarchy the reader is actually shown and the one the back-link on the page offers. A flat page (/timeline/, /channel/, /search/) gets no breadcrumb: a one-item trail states nothing, and `/search/` and `/404.html` are excluded from the index anyway. PARAMETERS: parent_url (site-relative, e.g. "/papers/"), parent_name. Inherited from seo_head.html: seo_abs, seo_title. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign crumb_site = site.url | append: site.baseurl | append: "/" -%}
