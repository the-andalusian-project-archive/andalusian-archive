{%- comment -%} BreadcrumbList, emitted as its own ld+json block alongside the entity block (2026-09-27, on-page SEO pass). Emitted ONLY where the site's own navigation states the hierarchy. Every page in the site nav links to Home and to its collection, and every detail page carries a "Back to " link, so Home -> -> item is the hierarchy the reader is actually shown and the one the back-link on the page offers. A flat page (/timeline/, /channel/, /search/) gets no breadcrumb: a one-item trail states nothing, and `/search/` and `/404.html` are excluded from the index anyway. PARAMETERS: parent_url (site-relative, e.g. "/papers/"), parent_name. Inherited from seo_head.html: seo_abs, seo_title. 2026-09-28. `parent_url` became OPTIONAL, and omitting it emits a TWO-item trail (Home -> this page) instead of three. The rule above says a flat page gets no breadcrumb, and that is still true of /timeline/, /channel/ and /search/ - all of which are unlinked utility or record pages with no second level to name. The two pages added on 2026-09-28 are a different case and the difference is worth stating rather than glossing: /asadullah-ali-al-andalusi/ and /asadullahali-com-what-happened/ are named entry points. The site's own nav and footer now publish them as such, they are the destinations a reader arriving from a search engine is meant to land on, and each has a Home link above it like every other page. A single-item trail - Home alone - would state nothing at all, which is why it is still rejected; but a two-item trail states the one thing that is true and non-trivial about a top-level page: it is a destination, not a child of some other part of the site. No third level is invented for either. There is no parent page to name, and naming a section that does not exist as a URL would be a breadcrumb that points nowhere. A middle ListItem with a name and no `item` would also be legal, but it would have to name something - and the only candidate is a footer heading, which is a place on a page, not a place in the site. Inventing a level to satisfy a pattern is the failure mode this file exists to prevent. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign crumb_site = site.url | append: site.baseurl | append: "/" -%} {%- if include.parent_url and include.parent_url != "" -%} {%- else -%} {%- endif -%}
