I will be giving a lecture on Liberalism in Muslim Thought come October 17th, in Sarawak Malaysia. There I will be confronting many new liberal organizations on their major university campuses, as well as how Liberalism affects Muslim societies. I will also be detailing that the major impact of Liberalism has been the rise of Muslim extremists, such as ISIS.
