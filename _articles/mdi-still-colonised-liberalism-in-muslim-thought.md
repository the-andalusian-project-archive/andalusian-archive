## Text

On May 9th, 2015 I gave a lecture on Liberalism in Muslim Thought at IMEC 2015 in Kuala Lumpur Malaysia. The lecture touched up on some of the basic principles of Liberalism and the three major effects it has on Muslim-majority societies at large: (1) The degradation of Islam, (2) The rise of atheism and Western exceptionalism, and (3) the rise of terrorism and extremism.
