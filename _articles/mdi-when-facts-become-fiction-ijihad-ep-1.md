## Text

In this web series called ‘iJihad’ (i.e. Intellectual Jihad), Br. Asadullah Ali Al-Andalusi analyzes and deconstructs popular arguments against Islam and the Muslim world made by critics and Islamophobes alike. In the first episode he looks at some claims made by Dutch Youtuber ‘Gryffix’, who essentially argues that Islamic fundamentalism leads Muslims to justify and support the violent imposition of Islam on to others – and in many cases– even leads them directly to violence. However, the evidences she uses in support of her claims don’t match up.
