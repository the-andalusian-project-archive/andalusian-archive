## Text

Shaykh Muhammad Fatha ibn Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn al-Husayn Ibn Nasir ibn ‘Amr ad-Dar’i al Aghlabi (d. 1085 AH), one of the most prominent scholars in Islamic history, had recited a dua, which is now known as the “Nasiri Dua” or “The Sword of Ibn Nasir”. It is a relevant and powerful dua for the contemporary Muslim community. Its recent publication has been undertaken as a response to Shaykh Abdalqadir as-Sufi’s desire that this be collectively recited and revived across the Muslim world as a proven means of calling upon Allah’s Generosity and for overcoming the enemies of Islam.
